RESIDENTIAL RENTAL AGREEMENT

This agreement is made between Mr. Ravi Kumar (the Landlord) and Ms. Anita Rao (the Tenant) for the flat at 12, Lake View Apartments.

1. Rent. The Tenant shall pay a monthly rent of Rs. 18,000 on or before the 5th day of every month.

2. Security deposit. The Tenant has paid a refundable security deposit of Rs. 54,000. The Landlord may deduct reasonable charges from the deposit at the time of vacating, and the balance will be returned in due course.

3. Lock-in period. The Tenant shall not vacate the flat during the first 6 months of the tenancy.

4. Termination by Tenant. After the lock-in period, the Tenant may end this agreement by giving 2 months' written notice to the Landlord.

5. Termination by Landlord. The Landlord may terminate this agreement at his sole discretion if he considers the Tenant's conduct unsuitable.

6. Maintenance. The Tenant shall pay maintenance charges as decided by the Landlord from time to time.

7. Late payment. If rent is not paid by the due date, a penalty may be levied on the Tenant.

8. Subletting. The Tenant shall not sublet or share the flat with any other person without the written consent of the Landlord.

9. Repairs. Minor repairs up to Rs. 1,000 shall be borne by the Tenant. Major repairs shall be borne by the Landlord.
