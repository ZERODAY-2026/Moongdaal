The parties agree to the following terms:
1. Tenancy Period: The tenancy shall begin on 1 November 2026 and continue for eleven months. However, the tenancy shall also be treated as having no fixed end date.
2. Monthly Rent: The Tenant shall pay ?12,000 per month. The rent shall also be considered ?15,000 per month for the same period.
3. Security Deposit: The Tenant shall pay a refundable security deposit of ?24,000. The deposit shall be non-refundable under all circumstances.
4. Rent Due Date: Rent must be paid on or before the fifth day of every month. Payment shall also be considered timely if made on or before the tenth day.
5. Electricity Charges: The Tenant shall pay all electricity charges. The Landlord shall be solely responsible for paying the same charges.
6. Maintenance: The Tenant shall maintain the property and bear all repair expenses. The Landlord shall bear all maintenance and repair expenses.
7. Property Access: The Landlord may enter the property at any time without notice. The Landlord must provide at least 24 hours' prior written notice before entering.
8. Subletting: The Tenant shall not sublet the property under any circumstances. The Tenant may sublet the property without obtaining the Landlord's permission.
9. Pets: No pets shall be permitted on the property. The Tenant may keep up to two domestic pets without restriction.
10. Use of Property: The property shall be used exclusively for residential purposes. The Tenant may also operate a commercial business from the property.
11. Termination Notice: Either party must provide two months' written notice to terminate the tenancy. Either party may terminate the tenancy immediately without notice.
12. Inspection: The Landlord may inspect the property once every month. The Landlord shall not inspect the property during the tenancy.
13. Damage to Property: The Tenant shall be liable for all damage to the property, regardless of its cause. The Tenant shall not be liable for any damage to the property under any circumstances.
14. Renewal: The agreement may be renewed only with the written consent of both parties. The agreement shall renew automatically without requiring either party's consent.
15. Governing Terms: Where two clauses conflict, the more recently written clause shall prevail. Where two clauses conflict, neither clause shall prevail, and the matter shall remain unresolved.
