# Private Legal Document Triage Agent

> **Problem Statement (PS2, Agentic AI, ZeroDay OSS Hacktoberfest 2026, IIIT Dharwad):**
> Legal documents such as rental agreements, employment contracts, legal notices and service agreements contain complex terminology and clauses that ordinary people find hard to understand, and professional help for every first question is not always practical. Build a **privacy-first, fully on-device AI agent** powered by a Gemma 3n-class model that can read legal documents, explain legal terminology in plain language, summarise key clauses, and highlight potential risks or ambiguities, **without presenting its explanations as definitive legal advice.**

**This tool is for preliminary document understanding and issue identification. It is not a lawyer and not a source of legal advice, and it cannot decide whether a clause is valid or enforceable.**

Everything runs on your own device: the document, extracted text, chunks, embeddings, questions and answers never leave your machine during normal operation.
